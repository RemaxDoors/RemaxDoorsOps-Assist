# How to raise an NCR

Five steps. It takes about two minutes.

---

## Step 1 — Open Add NCR

On the dashboard, press the red **Add NCR** button, top right.

The wizard has four stages along the top: **Job → Details → Photos → Assign**.
You can press **Back** at any point without losing what you have typed.

---

## Step 2 — Say which job it is against

Choose one of the three boxes:

| Box | Use it when | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Simpro job** | You have a Simpro job number | Type the number, press **Fetch job**. The customer, site and job details fill in by themselves. |
| **M1 job** | You have an M1 job number | Pick the job type, type three or more characters, then press **Use job** on the result. The part number fills in too. |
| **No job** | It is not job-related | Nothing else to enter. |

A job is optional — an NCR can be raised without one.

Press **Continue**.

---

## Step 3 — Describe what went wrong

**Description** is required. Say what is non-conforming, where you found it, and
against what it should have been.

To save typing, tap one of the grey shortcut buttons under the box — *Missing
parts*, *Damaged parts*, *Motor fault* and so on. Each one drops in an opening
line with the specifics in square brackets. **Replace anything in [brackets]
with the real detail.** You can tap more than one.

> Tap: `Damaged parts: [part, damage and location].`
> Make it read: `Damaged parts: 2x 3500 panel, scratched across the face, found on the truck at unload.`

Then fill in what you know:

- **Category** — required. Pick the closest one.
- **Code** and **Cause** — optional.
- **Which part / What is it called / How many affected** — leave the part blank if it is not a stocked item.
- **How serious is it** — your best judgement. It can be changed later.
- **Hours spent putting it right** and **Extra cost ($)** — leave at 0 if the work has not been done.

The **NCR ID** shown top right is the number this will get in M1.

Press **Continue**.

---

## Step 4 — Add photos

Press **Take photo** to use the camera, or **Photos or files** to attach
something already on the device. Add as many as are useful.

Photos are the single most useful thing you can add — whoever picks this up
next was not standing where you are.

Each file must be under 15MB. If one is too big it will be named and skipped,
and the rest still save.

Press **Continue**. You can skip this step if you have nothing to attach.

---

## Step 5 — Say who owns it, and save

- **Reported by** — required. This is already set to you. Change it only if you are entering this on someone else's behalf.
- **Assigned to** — pick a name from the team shortcuts underneath, or use the dropdown for anyone else. It can be left empty if you do not know yet.

Check the **Before you save** summary: NCR ID, category, part, job and the
number of attachments.

Tick **Raise a Simpro task as a reminder** if you want someone told to review
it. The task window opens once the NCR is saved.

Press **Save**. The NCR number comes back on screen, and the record is in M1.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **A red message when you save** — read it first; it usually says exactly what is missing.
- **"Your sign-in has expired"** — reload the page, sign in, and press Save again.
- **"M1 is not reachable"** — the NCR is held and goes in by itself when M1 is back. You will get the number then.
- **Anything else** — open the **Support** page from the sidebar. It shows what is working and sends a report with the detail already filled in. Attach a screenshot to that email.
